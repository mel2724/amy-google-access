Amy Read Only

A private assistant that helps its owner find and read information in Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets.

What Amy does

Amy searches files, retrieves document content, and reads spreadsheet data for the account owner’s requested tasks. Google access is provided through Composio.

Read-only access

The connection requests permission to see and download Drive files, read Docs documents, read Sheets spreadsheets, and identify the authorized account by email. It cannot create, edit, delete, move, or share Google files.

Personal use

This integration is operated by Melissa Sands for her personal assistant. This site provides app information. It does not offer public sign-up, collect Google credentials, or publish connected files.

Your control

Google permission can be revoked at any time in Google Account connections. Read the privacy information before authorizing access.
